Understanding ROI in Advertising
Return on Investment (ROI) is the ultimate measure of success in advertising.

Return on Investment (ROI) is the ultimate measure of success in advertising. Whether you’re running a digital ad campaign or a billboard promotion, understanding ROI ensures that your marketing dollars are well-spent.

Calculating ROI is straightforward: subtract your ad spend from your generated revenue, then divide by the ad spend. For example, if you spend $1,000 on an ad campaign and generate $4,000 in sales, your ROI is 300%. High ROI means your campaign is performing well, but tracking metrics like engagement rates, click-through rates, and conversions provides deeper insights.
